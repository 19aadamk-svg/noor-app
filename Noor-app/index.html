Sunnah & Voluntary Prayers
Fard times are matched to Tawakkulia Jamia Masjid's own timetable: a 15° angle for Fajr, the standard solar-noon Dhuhr, the Hanafi shadow-length factor for Asr, exact sunset for Maghrib, and Isha set 60 minutes after Maghrib rather than by twilight angle — mirroring how this specific mosque calculates its own "Begins" times. Jama'ah (congregation) times are set separately by the mosque and are usually a bit later than the times shown here.
